Remove hidden photo data, including GPS location, camera model, and the date and time, before sharing a photo. Your photo is cleaned on your device and never uploaded.

How It Works

  1. Choose a Photo: Select or drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Keep the Format: The converter saves a new copy in the same format by default, containing only the picture.
  3. Download: Save the clean copy, and share it instead of the original.

Key Features

  • Removes All Metadata: The new file is built from the pixels alone, so EXIF, GPS, and other hidden data are left behind.
  • Private by Design: Cleaning photos would be pointless if they were uploaded, so nothing leaves your device.
  • Same Format: Keeps your photo as a JPG, PNG, or WebP unless you choose otherwise.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EXIF data?

Information cameras and phones save inside photos, such as the camera model, settings, date and time, and often the exact GPS location where the photo was taken.

Why remove it before sharing?

A photo's GPS location can reveal where you live or work. Some sites remove this data when you upload, but email, messaging apps, and many websites keep it.

Does removing EXIF change my photo?

The picture is saved again, so JPG and WebP photos go through compression once more. Choose Best (95) to keep nearly all detail. One side effect: phones sometimes record rotation in EXIF, and the cleaned copy is saved the way your browser shows the photo, so it keeps the right way up.
