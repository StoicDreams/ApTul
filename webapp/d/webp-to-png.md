## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### Why convert WebP to PNG?

Some older apps, editors, and upload forms do not accept WebP. PNG works almost everywhere and keeps the image exactly as it is.

### Why is the PNG so much bigger?

PNG stores every pixel without losing detail, while WebP usually uses lossy compression. A larger file is expected and does not mean anything is wrong.

### Can I convert animated WebP images?

Only the first frame is converted, because PNG images here are still images.
