## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### How much smaller will my photo be?

It depends on the photo. At the same visible quality, WebP is usually 25 to 35 percent smaller than JPG, and lower quality settings save more.

### Which quality setting should I use?

Good (80) works for most photos on websites. Use High (90) for photography portfolios, and Smaller (65) for thumbnails.

### Will converting again lose more quality?

Each lossy conversion removes a little more detail, so convert from your original photo rather than from an already converted copy.
