Convert WebP images to PNG so you can open and edit them in any app. PNG keeps every pixel and any transparency. Nothing is uploaded.

How It Works

  1. Choose a WebP: Select a WebP image, or drop it onto the converter.
  2. Resize (Optional): Set a width or height if you need a different size.
  3. Download: Save the PNG file, named after your original image.

Key Features

  • Lossless: PNG keeps every pixel of the WebP exactly as it is.
  • Keeps Transparency: See-through areas stay see-through.
  • No Upload: Your image is converted on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert WebP to PNG?

Some older apps, editors, and upload forms do not accept WebP. PNG works almost everywhere and keeps the image exactly as it is.

Why is the PNG so much bigger?

PNG stores every pixel without losing detail, while WebP usually uses lossy compression. A larger file is expected and does not mean anything is wrong.

Can I convert animated WebP images?

Only the first frame is converted, because PNG images here are still images.
