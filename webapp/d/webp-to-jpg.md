## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### What happens to transparency?

JPG cannot store see-through areas, so they are filled with white. Convert to PNG instead if you need to keep transparency.

### Why would I need a JPG instead of WebP?

Some older software, printing services, and upload forms only accept JPG or PNG. JPG files are also smaller than PNG for photos.

### Is JPG the same as JPEG?

Yes. They are two names for the same format. The .jpg extension comes from older systems that only allowed three letters.
