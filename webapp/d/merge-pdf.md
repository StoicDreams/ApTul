## How It Works

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">
1. **Add Your PDFs**: Choose two or more files, or drop them onto the tool. Each shows its page count.
1. **Put Them in Order**: Use the arrows to move files up or down. The first file's pages come first.
1. **Merge**: Select Merge PDFs, and the combined file downloads as merged.pdf.
</webui-page-segment>

### Key Features

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">
- **Private**: Files are combined on your device. Nothing is uploaded, so it is safe for contracts, statements, and medical records.
- **No Limits**: Merge as many files as you like, of any size your device can handle, with no account and no watermark.
- **Keeps Every Page as It Was**: Page sizes, rotation, text, and images are copied without being converted, so nothing loses quality.
</webui-page-segment>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDFs are read and combined by your browser, and the merged file is saved straight to your device.

### Will the merged PDF keep links, bookmarks, and form fields?

Page content, including text, images, and web links on the page, is kept. Bookmarks and fillable form fields from the original files are not carried over, since they belong to each original document rather than to its pages.

### Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

PDFs that need a password to open cannot be merged here. PDFs that open without a password but have editing restrictions can be, and the merged file has no restrictions.

### How do I merge only some pages?

Use [Split PDF](/split-pdf) to take the pages you need from each file first, then merge those files.
