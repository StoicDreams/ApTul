## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### Will converting to PNG improve my photo?

No. Detail the JPG already lost cannot be recovered. PNG only stops any more from being lost when you edit and save again.

### Why is the PNG so much larger?

PNG is lossless, which takes much more space for photos. That is expected. Use JPG or WebP for sharing photos online.

### Can I make the background transparent?

Converting does not remove backgrounds. The PNG can hold transparency, but you need an image editor to erase the background.
