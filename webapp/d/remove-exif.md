## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### What is EXIF data?

Information cameras and phones save inside photos, such as the camera model, settings, date and time, and often the exact GPS location where the photo was taken.

### Why remove it before sharing?

A photo's GPS location can reveal where you live or work. Some sites remove this data when you upload, but email, messaging apps, and many websites keep it.

### Does removing EXIF change my photo?

The picture is saved again, so JPG and WebP photos go through compression once more. Choose Best (95) to keep nearly all detail. One side effect: phones sometimes record rotation in EXIF, and the cleaned copy is saved the way your browser shows the photo, so it keeps the right way up.
