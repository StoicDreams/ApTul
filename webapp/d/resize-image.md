## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### Can I make an image bigger?

Yes, up to 5,000 pixels, but enlarging cannot add detail that is not in the original, so large increases look soft.

### What size should images be for a website?

Make them no wider than they are shown. Around 1,600 to 2,000 pixels wide suits full-width images on large screens, and 800 to 1,200 pixels suits images within an article.

### Does resizing reduce quality?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. JPG and WebP images are saved again at the quality you choose, so pick High (90) or Best (95) to keep the most detail.
