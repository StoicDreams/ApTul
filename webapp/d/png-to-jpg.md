## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### Should I convert every PNG to JPG?

No. Keep logos, icons, screenshots of text, and images with transparency as PNG, where they stay sharp. JPG is best for photos and images with smooth color changes.

### What happens to the transparent parts of my PNG?

JPG has no transparency, so they are filled with white. The converter tells you when this happens.

### Why does text look blurry in the JPG?

JPG compression softens sharp edges. Choose Best (95), or keep text-heavy images as PNG.
