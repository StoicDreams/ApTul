## Frequently Asked Questions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

### What is lorem ipsum?

Placeholder text based on a passage from Cicero, written in 45 BC, with words changed and removed so it reads as nonsense. Designers use it so people judge a layout without being distracted by its words.

### Should I use placeholder text in a finished site?

No. Replace it before publishing, since search engines see lorem ipsum as low quality content and visitors find it confusing.

### Why use lorem ipsum instead of real text?

It has a natural mix of word lengths, so it fills space the way real text does, and nobody stops to read it.
