Convert JPG images to PNG to edit them without losing more quality each time you save. Your images are converted in your browser and never uploaded.

How It Works

  1. Choose a JPG: Select a JPG or JPEG image, or drop it onto the converter.
  2. Resize (Optional): Set a width or height if needed.
  3. Download: Save the PNG file, named after your original image.

Key Features

  • No Further Loss: PNG saves the image exactly, so editing and saving again does not add blur.
  • Removes Photo Data: Camera details and GPS location are not copied.
  • Private: Your photo stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will converting to PNG improve my photo?

No. Detail the JPG already lost cannot be recovered. PNG only stops any more from being lost when you edit and save again.

Why is the PNG so much larger?

PNG is lossless, which takes much more space for photos. That is expected. Use JPG or WebP for sharing photos online.

Can I make the background transparent?

Converting does not remove backgrounds. The PNG can hold transparency, but you need an image editor to erase the background.
