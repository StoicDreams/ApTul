Easily resize and convert images locally. Securely strips hidden metadata (EXIF/GPS) while converting to WebP, PNG, and JPG. Fast, private, and free.

This tool allows you to instantly resize and convert images into modern web formats without uploading your files to a server. Powered by high-performance Rust WebAssembly, your images are processed securely within your own browser, ensuring maximum privacy and speed.

How It Works

  1. Select Your Image: Choose any PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, ICO, or BMP file from your device.
  2. View File Details: Instantly see your image's current dimensions (width & height) and file size.
  3. Resize (Optional): Maintain the original size or scale your image by setting a custom Width or Height. Aspect ratio is preserved automatically to prevent distortion.
  4. Convert: Select your desired output format (WebP, JPG, PNG, BMP, or ICO).
  5. Download: Preview the converted file and download it instantly.

Key Features

  • Browser-Based Processing: Unlike other tools that upload your photos to a cloud server, our tool runs locally on your device. This means your images never leave your computer, guaranteeing 100% data privacy.
  • High Performance: Built with Rust and WebAssembly (Wasm), this tool delivers near-native speed for resizing and encoding images.
  • Modern Format Support: Convert standard JPEGs and PNGs into highly optimized WebP files to improve your website's loading speed and SEO scores.
  • Smart Resizing: High-quality Lanczos3 filtering ensures your resized images remain sharp and clear.
  • Automatic Metadata Stripping: When you take a photo with your phone, it often hides sensitive data like GPS coordinates, time stamps, and device info inside the file. Our tool automatically strips this "EXIF" data during conversion, ensuring your images are completely private and safe to share publicly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs in your browser using WebAssembly, so your images never leave your device.

Does converting remove EXIF and GPS data?

Yes. The image is decoded and saved as a new file that holds only the picture itself, so camera details, location, and dates from the original are not carried over.

Which format is best for websites?

WebP is usually the best choice. It makes smaller files than JPG or PNG at similar quality, and every current browser supports it. Use PNG when you need sharp edges with transparency, and JPG when a file must open everywhere.

Can I make a favicon?

Yes. Choose ICO as the format and resize the image to 256 pixels or smaller, such as 32 or 48 pixels wide.
