Convert PNG images to JPG to make photos and large screenshots much smaller for email and uploads. Choose the quality, then download. Nothing is uploaded.

How It Works

  1. Choose a PNG: Select a PNG image, or drop it onto the converter.
  2. Pick a Quality: Good (80) is a good balance for photos.
  3. Download: Save the JPG file, named after your original image.

Key Features

  • Much Smaller Photos: A PNG photo can shrink to a fraction of its size as a JPG.
  • White Background: Transparent areas are filled with white instead of black.
  • Private: Your image is converted on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I convert every PNG to JPG?

No. Keep logos, icons, screenshots of text, and images with transparency as PNG, where they stay sharp. JPG is best for photos and images with smooth color changes.

What happens to the transparent parts of my PNG?

JPG has no transparency, so they are filled with white. The converter tells you when this happens.

Why does text look blurry in the JPG?

JPG compression softens sharp edges. Choose Best (95), or keep text-heavy images as PNG.
